About

This project develops an AI literacy toolkit centered on a curated knowledge base — a structured corpus of readings, annotations, and concepts that frames AI as an infrastructural system spanning material resources, data ecosystems, governance, and lived experience.

Using this corpus as grounding context, the project explores how LLMs can function as tools for structuring, translating, and interrogating complex material. The outcome is both a designed knowledge base usable by students working with LLM tools, and a teaching toolkit that translates critical scholarship into assignments, modules, and workflows.

The corpus is plain text, model-agnostic, and open.
